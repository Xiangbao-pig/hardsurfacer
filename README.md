## 关于我们

ZIC Archive 收录经创作者授权的硬表面及相关概念设计作品，关注创意、审美、专业实践及行业协作。档案由 ZIC 发现、整理与编辑，主理人、策展与编辑为 [Jinyi Zhu](https://hardsurfacer.com/people/jinyi-zhu/)。

ZIC Archive 是档案网站的名称，ZIC 是其编辑与策展标识，Hardsurfacer 是其官网域名标识。本仓库是官方公开介绍与内容导航；作品、人物与文章的完整资料以官网页面为准。

## About

ZIC Archive is an independent editorial archive centered on hard-surface design. It presents work with the creators’ permission and documents ideas, aesthetics, professional practice and collaboration. The archive is curated and edited by ZIC, with [Jinyi Zhu](https://hardsurfacer.com/people/jinyi-zhu/) as its curator and editor.

Hardsurfacer identifies the archive’s official domain, https://hardsurfacer.com/. This repository provides an official introduction and a directory of public resources. Complete work descriptions, artist profiles and editorial content are available on the website.

- [Works](https://hardsurfacer.com/en/works/)
- [People](https://hardsurfacer.com/en/people/)
- [Interviews](https://hardsurfacer.com/en/interviews/)
- [How Do You Become a Hard-Surface Designer?](https://hardsurfacer.com/en/interviews/how-to-become-a-hard-surface-designer/)

English detail pages are published as translations complete. Other entries remain available in their original language.
